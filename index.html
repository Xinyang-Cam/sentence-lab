03 / UNDER THE HOOD

From language to numbers.

“I think…”→[tokens]
01

Encode

A sentence transformer reads each sentence and represents it as a vector of 384 numbers.

02

Compare

Cosine similarity measures the angle between two vectors. Closer directions generally suggest greater semantic relatedness.

≈≠
03

Interpret carefully

A high score does not prove that the author’s stance, nuance, or factual meaning is unchanged—especially with negation.

READ THIS LIKE A RESEARCHER.Scores are cosine values, not percentages or probabilities. The vectors are 384-dimensional; the diagram shows their mathematically exact angle, not a 2D projection. Browser inference uses a quantized ONNX version of the same pretrained model, so results may differ slightly from the original Python analysis. The dimensional stripes are not individually interpretable semantic features.
